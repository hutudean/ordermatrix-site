Shopify-native B2B workflow

Turn messy B2B purchase requests into Shopify-validated Draft Orders and RFQs.

OrderMatrix B2B is being developed as a focused workflow layer for structured review, validation, and exception handling. It is not open for public use and must not be used with real procurement data during this private preview.

AI and parsers produce candidate rows only. Shopify remains the source of truth for product identity, company context, publication, price, currency, quantity rules, payment terms, and transaction state.

A deliberately narrow workflow

The private preview concentrates on three connected parts of the purchasing journey.

01

Smart Intake

Convert supported purchase-request inputs into candidate product rows for buyer or merchant review. No parser output is trusted as a final order decision.

02

Shopify-native validation

Re-check candidates against Shopify company, catalog, contextual pricing, currency, publication, and quantity rules before a transaction can proceed.

03

Exception recovery

Preserve unmatched or blocked rows for review and use an RFQ path when a request cannot safely become a Shopify Draft Order.

Private-preview status

OrderMatrix B2B is undergoing platform, privacy, and workflow validation. There is no public installation link or application form. Availability will be communicated only after the required evidence and reviews are complete.
