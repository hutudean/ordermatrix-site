Private Preview Privacy Notice

Effective and last updated: August 20, 2026

Private-preview boundary OrderMatrix B2B is not publicly available or listed on the Shopify App Store. Do not use the preview with real customer information, procurement files, or production store data. Platform, privacy, and legal reviews are not represented as complete by this notice.

1. Scope

This notice describes how Juan Studio ("we", "us", or "our") handles information on this public informational website and in an expressly authorized OrderMatrix B2B private-preview environment.

A Shopify merchant is responsible for the customer and commerce data in its store. Shopify remains the authoritative platform for product, company, catalog, publication, contextual price, currency, quantity rule, payment-term, Draft Order, and Order information.

2. Public website

This website is a static information site hosted by GitHub Pages. It has no contact form, account registration, advertising, tracking pixel, or analytics script. The pages do not set application cookies or use browser storage.

GitHub may process ordinary network and hosting information when it serves these pages under its own terms and privacy practices. If you choose the email link, your email provider and Gmail process the address, message, headers, and attachments you send.

Do not email customer information, order content, procurement files, credentials, access tokens, or other sensitive data.

3. Data the private-preview app may process

Only an expressly authorized synthetic or anonymized test environment may use the preview. Depending on the workflow being tested, the app may process:

  • Shopify shop, installation, session, entitlement, and configuration information.
  • Shopify customer identifiers and authenticated buyer context; company, company-location, and contact context; and related names or contact details when supplied by Shopify.
  • Product, variant, publication, contextual price, currency, quantity rule, payment-term, Draft Order, and order-history data needed to validate a test purchasing workflow.
  • Candidate line items, quantities, purchase-order references, notes, quote and RFQ records, bounded validation evidence, and operational identifiers needed for intake, exception handling, auditability, and duplicate-mutation protection.
  • Shop-scoped aggregate allowance, provider-attempt, and rate-limit records that do not intentionally store raw purchase-request content.

Full purchase-request text, email text, images, PDFs, prompts, and filenames are intended to remain transient. The app may retain only bounded, sanitized candidate and workflow evidence needed for review, recovery, or auditing.

4. How the preview uses data

  • Authenticate and scope the preview to the correct Shopify shop.
  • Convert supported test inputs into candidate rows and present them for human review.
  • Validate candidate rows against the current Shopify B2B context.
  • Preserve exception, RFQ, quote, and Draft Order workflow state with bounded audit evidence.
  • Prevent duplicate external mutations, enforce usage allowances and rate limits, troubleshoot safe error codes, and fulfill verified Shopify privacy requests.

Parser and AI output is candidate-only. It does not establish trusted product identity, eligibility, price, discount, payment terms, or a final order decision.

6. Optional Google Gemini processing

AI intake is default-off and requires an authorized merchant to enable it and acknowledge the current disclosure. When enabled, selected synthetic or anonymized test text, email text, images, or PDFs may be sent to Google Gemini for candidate parsing. Inputs can contain personal or sensitive business data if a user includes it, so unnecessary information must be removed before submission.

Requests ask that the interaction not be stored for later API retrieval. That setting is not a zero-data-retention guarantee and does not replace provider, contractual, security, or legal review. Real merchant or buyer content must not be sent during the private preview.

7. Browser storage in the preview buyer experience

The preview may use browser storage to keep a buyer from losing work. Browser values are convenience state only and are revalidated by the server before use.

Data Storage and duration Boundary
Authenticated buyer draft localStorage, up to 7 days Namespaced to the Shopify shop and verified buyer.
Guest draft sessionStorage, current browser-tab session Removed when the tab session ends.
Company-location preference sessionStorage, current session Namespaced to shop and buyer; ownership is rechecked by the server.
Recent search terms Up to 8 entries in localStorage for up to 30 days; guests use the current session Namespaced to shop and verified buyer when authenticated.
Submission request identifiers sessionStorage, until a definitive outcome Used to reduce accidental duplicate submissions.

Draft content is transmitted to the app server when the buyer asks to validate, request a quote, or submit it. Expired, incompatible, identity-mismatched, cleared, or successfully submitted local state is removed. Users should clear preview data before leaving a shared device.

8. Retention, privacy requests, and deletion

The app is designed to minimize retained purchase-request material, keep raw files and prompts transient, and retain bounded workflow records only while they are needed for the tested purchasing, security, audit, or privacy process. Final production retention and backup-deletion schedules have not been approved and are not claimed here.

The repository implements Shopify's required customer data-request, customer-redaction, and shop-redaction workflows. Actual external operation and response evidence remains part of private-preview validation. Shopify-native commerce records remain subject to the merchant's Shopify configuration and Shopify's own retention practices.

9. Security and current limitations

The application is designed to authenticate Shopify requests, scope data by shop and verified buyer context, fail closed on authorization and external-mutation uncertainty, avoid intentional logging of raw prompts or credentials, and use encrypted network connections where supported.

No security measure eliminates all risk. Production infrastructure, backup, monitoring, protected-customer-data, provider, and legal evidence is not represented as complete. This is why the product remains a private preview restricted to synthetic or anonymized test data.

10. Requests, changes, and contact

A buyer should normally direct a privacy request to the Shopify merchant whose store they used. Merchants and other visitors may ask about this notice or report a privacy concern at huan244194945@gmail.com. We aim to respond within 5 business days.

Put “Privacy Request” in the subject line and identify the relevant Shopify shop without including customer records, purchase files, credentials, or tokens in the email. We may update this notice when the preview's practices change; the effective date at the top will be updated at the same time.
