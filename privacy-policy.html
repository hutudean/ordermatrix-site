1 Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Juan Studio ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the OrderMatrix B2B Shopify application (the "App"). The App is available on the Shopify App Store and is designed to streamline B2B wholesale ordering for Shopify merchants and their business customers.

By installing or using the App, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. This policy applies to:

  • Merchants — Shopify store owners who install and configure the App.
  • Buyers — Business customers who use the quick order form embedded in the merchant's storefront.

If you have questions about this policy, please contact us at huan244194945@gmail.com.

2 Information We Collect

We collect information in three ways: (a) through the Shopify API, (b) directly from merchants through in-app configuration, and (c) directly from buyers through the storefront order form.

2.1 Data obtained via the Shopify API

When a merchant grants the App access during installation, we retrieve the following data from Shopify to provide the App's core functionality:

Data Type Shopify API Scope Purpose
Customer names, email addresses, and customer tags read_customers Apply customer-group pricing rules; associate orders with buyers; process wholesale applications
Order history (line items, amounts, fulfillment status) read_orders Display order history to buyers; enable one-click reorder
Draft orders write_draft_orders Create draft orders on behalf of buyers; support the merchant approval workflow
Product catalog (titles, SKUs, variants, inventory, images) Via Admin GraphQL Power the quick order search and product variant matrix
Online store navigation menus write_online_store_navigation Automatically inject a quick order link into the store's navigation
Company accounts (Shopify B2B) read_companies Support Shopify Plus B2B company-level pricing (read-only)

2.2 Data provided by merchants

Merchants directly enter the following data into the App's admin interface:

  • App settings (brand color, form title, product visibility rules, minimum order amounts)
  • Customer-group pricing rules and tiered discount configurations
  • Per-variant minimum order quantities (MOQ) and maximum purchase limits
  • Net Terms rules (payment terms per customer tag)
  • Auto-approval rules for draft orders
  • Quotes (price replies) sent to buyers in response to Request for Quote submissions

2.3 Data provided by buyers (storefront users)

Buyers interact with the App through the quick order form embedded in the merchant's storefront. The following data is collected directly from buyers:

  • Wholesale registration form: company name, contact person name, email address, phone number, website URL, tax ID / business registration number, estimated purchase volume, and an optional message.
  • Request for Quote (RFQ): the product list and quantities the buyer wishes to quote, along with any notes the buyer includes.
  • Saved lists (Favorites): product lists saved by the buyer for future reuse (stored against the buyer's Shopify customer ID).
  • Order notes: any free-text notes the buyer enters when submitting an order.

2.4 Automatically collected behavioral data

When a buyer uses the quick order form, the App automatically records behavioral events to power the merchant's analytics dashboard:

Event Data recorded
Product search Search query text, anonymous session ID, timestamp, shop domain
Add to order Product ID, variant ID, quantity, anonymous session ID, timestamp, shop domain
Order submitted Order amount, anonymous session ID, customer ID (if logged in), timestamp, shop domain
Order completed Order amount, customer ID (if logged in), timestamp, shop domain

The anonymous session ID is generated using crypto.randomUUID() and stored in sessionStorage (not cookies). It is not linked to any personally identifiable information unless the buyer is already logged in to the merchant's store.

3 How We Use the Information

We use the information we collect solely to provide and improve the App's features:

  • Quick order processing: Search for products, build order rows, and submit draft orders to Shopify on behalf of buyers.
  • Pricing calculation: Apply customer-group discounts and tiered pricing rules based on the buyer's customer tags and order quantities.
  • Order approval workflow: Present pending draft orders to merchants for review, and complete or reject them through the Shopify Admin.
  • Request for Quote: Store buyer RFQ submissions and allow merchants to respond with a quoted price.
  • Net Terms: Determine which payment terms apply to a given buyer based on their customer tags.
  • Wholesale registration: Store and present registration applications for merchant review and approval.
  • Analytics dashboard: Aggregate behavioral events to show the merchant a conversion funnel, top products, popular search terms, and daily trends.
  • Saved lists: Allow buyers to save and reload frequently ordered product lists.
  • Order history & reorder: Display the buyer's past orders and facilitate reordering.

We do not use collected information for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to the operation of the App.

4 Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade any personal information to third parties. We share data only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Shopify: The App operates within the Shopify platform. Data retrieved via the Shopify API is used exclusively to fulfill requests back to that same Shopify store. All communication with Shopify is governed by Shopify's Privacy Policy.
  • Infrastructure providers: The App is hosted on cloud infrastructure in the United States. Hosting providers process data only as needed to run the App and are bound by confidentiality obligations.
  • Legal requirements: We may disclose information if required to do so by law or in response to a valid legal process (e.g., court order or subpoena).
  • Business transfer: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, data may be transferred to the acquiring party, who will be bound by this Privacy Policy.

5 Data Storage and Security

All App data (pricing rules, draft order records, wholesale applications, quotes, saved lists, analytics events, and Shopify OAuth session tokens) is stored in a PostgreSQL database hosted in the United States.

We implement the following security measures:

  • Encryption in transit: All data transmitted between the App, Shopify, and buyers is encrypted using HTTPS / TLS.
  • Access control: The App server runs as a non-root user; database credentials are stored as environment variables, not in source code.
  • API rate limiting: All public-facing API endpoints enforce per-store rate limits to prevent abuse.
  • Security headers: The App sets X-Content-Type-Options, X-Frame-Options, Referrer-Policy, and Permissions-Policy headers on all responses.
  • Webhook verification: All Shopify webhook payloads are verified using HMAC-SHA256 before processing.

While we take reasonable precautions, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. If you believe your data has been compromised, please contact us immediately at huan244194945@gmail.com.

7 Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We do not use cookies. The App does not set any first-party or third-party cookies.

The storefront quick order form uses sessionStorage (not cookies) to:

  • Store a randomly generated, anonymous session ID for the duration of the browser tab session. This ID is discarded when the tab is closed.
  • Temporarily persist draft order rows so buyers do not lose their work if the page is refreshed.

We also use localStorage to store search history (up to 8 recent queries) and, for unauthenticated buyers, saved product lists. This data is stored locally in the buyer's browser and is not transmitted to our servers.

We do not use any third-party analytics services (such as Google Analytics), advertising networks, or tracking pixels.

8 GDPR and Data Subject Rights

We comply with the Shopify app requirements for GDPR. We have implemented all three mandatory privacy webhooks required by Shopify:

  • customers/data_request — When a Shopify store receives a data access request from a customer, Shopify forwards it to us. We will compile and provide all data we hold for that customer to the requesting merchant within a reasonable timeframe.
  • customers/redact — Upon receiving a customer redaction request, we will delete all personally identifiable data we hold for that customer from our database.
  • shop/redact — Upon receiving a shop redaction request (typically 48 hours after app uninstallation), we will permanently delete all data associated with that store from our systems.

If you are a buyer and wish to exercise your rights under GDPR (access, rectification, erasure, portability, or objection), please contact the merchant whose store you used — they are the data controller for your order and personal information. You may also contact us directly at huan244194945@gmail.com and we will assist.
