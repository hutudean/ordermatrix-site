## 5. Current agreement evidence

We keep one necessary current acceptance record for each current installation. It contains the trusted shop reference, installation generation, accepted document-bundle version and hash, first successful server acceptance time, authenticated-administrator action source, explicit acceptance and declared authority. It is used for current agreement access and specific service or privacy questions. It is not used for marketing, cross-store analysis or a profile of the natural person who clicked.

This record does not collect a natural person's name, email, telephone, address, IP address, user agent, device fingerprint, signature, identity document, session token or purchasing content. This statement is limited to the acceptance record; it does not say that Shopify, infrastructure or other necessary service logs never handle technical identifiers.

Repeated acceptance of the same current version keeps the first successful timestamp. Successful explicit acceptance of a new version replaces the prior record. If that action fails or its result is unknown, the old record is not first discarded or represented as a new acceptance. We do not keep a complete historical chain. A replaced or deleted record cannot establish whether an older version was accepted; public text archives establish document content only.

Trusted uninstall immediately invalidates acceptance for that installation. The record is removed in the same controlled handling step where possible, otherwise on the first successful run of the existing reliable deletion path. Reinstallation requires new acceptance. Verified enforceable deletion or end of necessity also triggers the applicable scoped deletion process. Deleting evidence does not create new acceptance. Applicable earlier obligations and the Addendum's deletion limit govern originals and copies.

## 8. Security and privacy requests

The App uses server-side store-scoped access checks and validates purchasing candidates before confirmed commerce actions. Optional AI has a separate setting and disclosure, plan limits and dispatch protections. AI suggestions do not establish product identity, price or a completed order. Unknown results are not automatically represented as success or resent. Scoped privacy handling uses installation boundaries, locks and generation checks to prevent stale work from affecting a newer installation. Privacy diagnostic events use restricted fields rather than raw purchasing content. Access to operational accounts is restricted and protected by two-step verification. Our recovery procedure requires restored copies to remain isolated until applicable later deletions have been reapplied and checked. The individual operator handles support and incident responsibilities under documented procedures. No system can guarantee that every incident is prevented. Applicable incident duties are handled separately from ordinary inquiry response targets.

Contact **support@juanllc.com** to initiate a privacy inquiry with a brief description. We will identify any proportionate verification of identity, authority and scope and an appropriate handling method; do not include credentials, identity documents or customer exports in the initial inquiry. Where the Merchant controls the requested data, we work through the relevant Merchant or platform process. Section 2 explains roles and available privacy rights; applicable complaint routes are listed there.
